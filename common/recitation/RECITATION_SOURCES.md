# Recitation tracking on the mushaf: sources and licenses

The mushaf's recitation tracking (`src/main/java/com/quran/recitation/tracking/`
`PhonemeFollower`, `LivePhonemeRecitation`, `ZipformerModel`, `RecitationPhonemes`) is this
project's own code. It uses:

- **Recognition model**: Quran-Lab's
  [zipformer_p-arabic-v3](https://huggingface.co/Quran-Lab/zipformer_p-arabic-v3), a streaming
  phoneme recognizer for Quran recitation, released under the Quran-Lab No-Profit License 1.2
  (NPL-1.2). It is downloaded at runtime, not part of the app; the copy downloaded is the
  Hugging Face file (SHA256 `6a5ddafa9c5e5c01260d30264031b341785bdc152e9ef1d569b41c8c278508eb`,
  72,705,392 bytes). The license forbids charging for the model, access to it or any feature it
  powers (advertising on such a feature included), and asks that copies of it and of anything
  derived from it keep its license file, under the same license. Access to the model was granted
  on also stating, in any application built on it, that automatic tajweed feedback can be wrong
  and does not replace a qualified teacher (the About screen's credit for the model says so, in
  those words), and not presenting its output as an authoritative religious ruling on anyone's
  recitation.

  Files from the model in this repository, under NPL-1.2 with its license file beside them:
  `src/main/assets/recitation/tokens.txt` (the model's list of phoneme units, shipped in the app)
  and the recognizer output recorded for the tests in `src/test/resources/zipformer/`.
- **Model runtime**: [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) (Apache-2.0), downloaded
  by `app/build.gradle.kts`.
- **Phonemes of the Quran text**: `src/main/assets/recitation/phonemes.txt`, generated by
  `tools/recitation-phonemes/export_phonemes.py` with
  [quran-transcript](https://github.com/obadx/quran-transcript) (MIT) from the
  [Tanzil](https://tanzil.net) Quran Text (Uthmani 1.1, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0; Tanzil is
  credited on the About screen).
